ORBITBILLS CAMERA FIX — HOW TO APPLY
====================================

1. Copy / merge:
   android/app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml  →  add CAMERA permission lines
   android/app/src/main/java/.../MainActivity.java  →  camera WebChromeClient grant

2. From your full OrbitBills Capacitor project root:

   npx cap sync android
   cd android && ./gradlew assembleDebug

3. Uninstall old APK on phone, install new debug APK.

4. Billing → Scan → Camera scan → Allow camera.

Full details: APPLY_CAMERA_FIX.md
